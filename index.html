Common questions

Email signature management, explained.

The questions companies ask most often before moving their email signatures to a managed setup.

Email signature management is the process of designing, deploying and updating email signatures across an entire organisation from one place, rather than asking each employee to set up their own.

It covers the signature design itself, rolling it out to every mailbox, keeping job titles and contact details accurate as people join or change roles, and updating campaign banners or legal disclaimers company-wide without touching individual devices.

Yes, but only if it is built for it. Each email client renders HTML differently: Outlook on Windows uses the Microsoft Word rendering engine, Gmail strips certain CSS, and Apple Mail handles images and scaling on its own terms.

We build signatures with table-based layouts and inline styles, then test them across Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail and mobile clients before deployment, so what you approve is what your recipients actually see.

Most standard designs are ready for approval within a few working days of receiving your brand assets and staff details. Once you approve the design, deployment across your team usually follows within the same week.

Custom designs take a little longer, since they involve additional revision rounds.

A standard branded signature design is £99 and a fully custom design is £199, both one-off. The optional management platform is £10 per user per year from the second user onwards, which covers centralised updates, banner changes and staff additions.

There is no long-term contract on the design work itself.

That is what the management platform is for. Campaign banners, disclaimers, new branding or a change of office address can be pushed to every mailbox centrally, without asking staff to edit anything themselves.

This matters most for marketing banners, which tend to change monthly, and for legal disclaimers, which need to be consistent across every message the company sends.

For a design-only order, no. We deliver the signature files along with installation instructions for each email client, and your team or IT provider installs them.

For centralised management, deployment method depends on whether you run Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and we will walk you through the options before anything is connected.
